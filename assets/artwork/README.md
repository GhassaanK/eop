# Editorial artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool as illustrations, not documentary images. Original PNGs are preserved here. Optimized WebP versions are in public/images and are rendered through next/image.

## Olive tree

Use case: illustration-story. Create one sophisticated editorial fine-art illustration for Echo of Palestine, a serious student solidarity publication. Subject: an old Palestinian olive tree with a gnarled trunk and individually observed olive leaves, rooted on quiet terraced land, distant low hills. Medium: authentic hand-carved woodcut / linocut printed on warm off-white uncoated paper, precise irregular engraved marks and restrained tactile grain, art-book quality, calm and dignified. Landscape composition 3:2, tree slightly right of center, spacious sky, no sun disc. Palette exclusively deep forest green #09573D ink, black ink, off-white #F8F8F5 paper; solid ink with hatching, no gradients. No text, typography, logos, flags, people, icons, SVG-like flat shapes, clipart, glossy digital effects, ornamental borders, fabricated documentary photography, or generic AI imagery. This is a narrative illustration, not a logo. Output a standalone artwork filling the frame.

## Study and olive

Use case: illustration-story. Standalone editorial art for a serious international student solidarity publication. A quiet study still life: two modest well-used clothbound books with blank spines, an open notebook with empty pages, and one natural olive twig resting across the notebook on a simple desk; no other props. Hand-carved woodcut / linocut print on warm off-white uncoated paper, irregular finely engraved hatching, subtle tactile paper grain, sophisticated art-book illustration. Restrained deep forest green #09573D and black ink on off-white #F8F8F5 only. Wide landscape composition 3:2, objects arranged naturally across lower two thirds with breathing room, slightly elevated viewpoint, believable book and page anatomy. Solid ink and hatching only, no gradients. No text, lettering, logos, flags, people, icons, SVG-like graphics, clipart, ornate borders, glossy effects, collage, or generic corporate illustration. Calm, thoughtful, human printmaking aesthetic, not an icon or logo.
